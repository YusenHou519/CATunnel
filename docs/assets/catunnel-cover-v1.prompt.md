## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create one finished illustrated cover for CATunnel, a lightweight macOS menu-bar app that connects remote servers through an SSH tunnel to the user's local proxy.
Canvas: a very wide horizontal panorama, approximately 2.5:1 (2560 by 1024 if possible), generous safe margins, suitable as a GitHub README cover.

Style reference: the user-attached panoramic gold-leaf East Asian landscape scroll. Reinterpret its visual language for CATunnel: warm ivory silk-paper texture; finely drawn charcoal-brown outlines; richly patterned ochre and antique gold-leaf mountains; horizontal decorative gold cloud bands; intricate wave and floral motifs; restrained crimson and ivory plum blossoms framing the two edges. Flat hand-painted decorative illustration, beautifully detailed but calm, like an antique painted folding screen. Matte paper, soft subtle gold foil, not shiny 3D.

Scene: along the lower half, a slim modern laptop rests on a low dark wooden scholar's desk at the left, its warm charcoal screen showing a few simple ivory code strokes. On the right, two small groups of unmistakable server cabinets, drawn with fine ink lines and muted ivory/charcoal surfaces, sit beneath elegant open-sided pavilion roofs integrated into the landscape. A graceful fine vermilion-and-gold connection line leads from the laptop into a clearly visible dark arched tunnel through the central gold mountain, emerges beyond the mountain and connects to the server groups. Make this continuous connection the visual narrative, clearly separated from ornamental cloud patterns. Small discreet status lights echo the vermilion blossom accents. Mountain ridges, patterned waves, plum blossoms and quiet clouds weave technology naturally into the classical scenery. No people are needed.

Composition: intricate panoramic landscape occupies the lower 55-60 percent; upper center has a generous clean ivory-paper field for a beautiful product title. Gold clouds stay mostly toward the edges and never cross the lettering. Keep the laptop, tunnel, and servers distinct and easily understood at reduced size. Balanced, spacious, premium editorial illustration, not a busy technical infographic.

Text, exactly and only these two lines:
"CATunnel"
"Coding Agent Tunnel"
CATunnel must have exactly this capitalization (C A T u n n e l). Put it prominently in the upper-center negative space in large elegant dark ink serif lettering, crisp and perfectly readable; subtitle much smaller with restrained spacing beneath it. Do not add any other text.

Avoid: real IP addresses, company logos, Apple logo, OpenAI/Claude logos, cats, lab equipment, scientific molecules, anatomical objects, chart labels, watermark, fake interface windows, neon, cyberpunk, photorealism, glossy 3D, aggressive metallic gradients. The result should feel closely related in material and decorative style to the reference, with a new original CATunnel composition.
